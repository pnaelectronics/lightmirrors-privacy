Light Mirrors Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Light Mirrors ("the game") is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Phong Bui ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. You don't need an account to play, and I don't ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: your current level, stars, remaining lives, sound and vibration settings, and whether you bought Remove Ads. This stays on your phone. I do not receive it. If you delete the game, it is removed.

Checking the time

To stop the daily lives refill from being skipped by changing the phone's clock, the game briefly asks a public web server (www.apple.com) for the current date and time. This is a normal web request. It sends no personal details beyond your IP address, which any website you visit receives.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Ads appear between levels, and as optional rewarded ads when you choose to watch one for a hint, extra taps or extra lives. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device identifiers (for example the advertising identifier, if you allow it), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before ads are loaded, and you can change your choice later in the game's Settings screen.

Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. On iPhone the game asks whether you allow it to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites to show more relevant ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown, but they are less personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Purchases

The game offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads", which is handled entirely by Apple. I do not see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game whether the purchase has been made, so it can be restored on a new device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

I do not sell your information. The only parties that receive data because of the game are Google (for ads) and Apple (for purchases and the time check), as described above.

Your choices

  • Say no, or later withdraw, consent for ads where you are asked.
  • Limit ad tracking in your iPhone settings.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop the ads shown between levels.
  • Delete the game to remove everything it stores on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
